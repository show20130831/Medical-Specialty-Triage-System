# Drill D3: Overload

**Date:** 2026-10-15 · **Operator:** project owner · **Result:** passed on the second
attempt; two findings

## Scenario

Drive `/predict` above the supported capacity for 15 minutes with k6 and confirm that
`TriageHighLatency` fires within 12 minutes, is delivered, and resolves after the load
stops. Requests are synthetic and marked `X-Synthetic-Traffic: 1`.

## Attempts

1. **k6 never ran.** Git Bash rewrote the container path `/loadtest/load.js` into a
   Windows path, so k6 exited immediately and the 18-minute watch measured nothing.
   Fixed by running with `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1`.
2. **No overload at 2 users.** p95 stayed at 0.46–0.49 s for the whole 15 minutes, below
   the 0.5 s threshold, so not firing was correct. The first capacity sweep had shown
   863 ms at 2 users; repeated runs showed the laptop's results vary (see
   `reports/load_test.md`). This attempt also raised `TriageDriftAlert`, because the load
   test's synthetic payloads entered the drift windows; they are now marked and
   excluded.
3. **Overload at 4 and 8 users** (this record).
